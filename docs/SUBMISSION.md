**Notes / Description (under 1000 characters):**

ClauseGuard is a standalone GenLayer Intelligent Contract for technical consistency checks across version-bound agreements, policies, SLAs, purchase orders and addenda. Validators independently retrieve commit-pinned documents, verify SHA-256 hashes and compare seven obligation areas under a locked policy and explicit precedence. Custom equivalence checks require matching evidence fingerprints and area classifications, then audit semantic support and completeness. Immutable records include quotations, policy commitments and decision hashes. Inputs and retries are bounded; invalid evidence or model output returns INCONCLUSIVE. A finalized StudioNet Full Consensus test detected conflicting delivery dates, exact fees and data-use duties, recognized compatible service minimums and ignored an embedded prompt injection. Includes 33 passing tests, GenVM validation, type checking and source-match evidence. Technical consistency only, not legal advice or binding interpretation.
